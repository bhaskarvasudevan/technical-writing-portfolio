### 2.0 Concepts 
This section lists key concepts that you need to understand to work with Zoom Workplace.

Online meetings 
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Meetings need not be in person any longer. _Members_ of a team or a group need not be physically present together in the same meeting room any longer to conduct a meeting. Meetings can be conducted __virtually__, on the internet. Members of a meeting can attend  meetings from their home, cafe, or office using their mobile phone or laptop using *collaboration* software (e.g., Zoom Workplace, Teams). Such meetings are **online meetings**.

***Video conferencing***
___Members of a meeting can___
__*see and*__
**_speak to_**
each other in an online meeting by turning on/off 
video and 
audio 
using collaboration software from their mobile phone or laptop. 
Such a video meeting is called video conferencing.

4.5 Join a meeting


Follow the steps below to join a meeting:
Click Join.


The below screen appears.

Enter the Meeting ID or select the meeting you want to join from the drop-down menu. 
Edit name shown if you desire. 
When you join the meeting, you will appear with this name. 
Select Don’t connect to audio if you want audio to be turned off when you join the meeting.
Select Turn off my video if you do not want to appear on video when you join the meeting. 
Click Join. 
Select Join with computer audio. 

You will be in the meeting. 
Click Unmute if you desire.

4.6 Schedule a meeting


Follow the below steps to schedule a meeting:
Click Schedule. The below screen appears.

Edit the meeting name if you desire.
Select the meeting start date and start time.
Select the meeting end date and end time. 
Select Repeat frequency as desired. The default value is Never.
Enter the email addresses of the meeting attendees.
Leave Enable Continuous Meeting Chat enabled if you desire. If you turn off this setting, meeting chat will be disabled.
Select one of the below for Meeting ID:
Generate Automatically. A random meeting ID will be generated by Zoom Workplace.
Personal Meeting ID.
Your personal meeting ID will be used as the meeting ID.
Add any attachments you want to send along with the meeting invite to meeting attendees.

Change Passcode for Meeting Security if you desire. By default, a random passcode is provided as seen in the screenshot.
Select Waiting Room if you want attendees to wait in a virtual lobby before they are admitted to the meeting.
Select Only authenticated users can join; Sign in to Zoom if you want attendees to have signed into Zoom before joining the meeting.
4.6 Actions in a meeting
Once you are in a meeting, you can perform various actions. The below screenshot shows the options available. These options are available for both 
the presenter and 
the participants 
of the meeting. 
However, some actions are available only to the presenter. e.g., the ability to
mute all participants in a meeting.
end a meeting. 








Audio: Click on Audio to turn audio on/off. If you turn off audio, you will be muted. You can still hear the others speak.
Video: Click on Video to turn video on/off. If you turn off video, you will not be seen in the meeting via video camera. You can still view others (if they have turned their video on) and hear others speak.
Participants: Click on Participants to view the participants in the meeting.
Chat: Chat with everyone in the meeting or privately with specific participants.
React: Post a reaction to the presenter of the meeting.
Share: Share your desktop or a window on your desktop with the meeting attendees. 
More: More actions you can perform.
End: End the meeting.
